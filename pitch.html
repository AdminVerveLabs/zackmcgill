An offer, if it is useful

If you are considering me, I would be glad to do a small piece of the job as part of the process.

Interviews are a good way to find out how someone talks about their work and a harder way to find out how they do it. So alongside whatever your process normally involves, I would like to offer something you can look at: a short first-quarter plan, written for your business, from a couple of conversations. This page explains what that would mean, why I am offering it, and what it would and would not involve.

In short

If it would be useful, I would be glad to put together a short first-quarter plan for you as part of the process. It would come out of one or two conversations about what is going well and what is getting in the way, and you would have something concrete of mine to react to rather than only my answers to questions. It is how I was hired at Black & White Zebra, and the plan I wrote then turned out to be roughly the one I ran. It needs nothing from your systems, and if it does not fit how you hire, that is completely fine.

Why I am offering this

I spent the last year learning what AI changes about this work, and came out of it wanting a team and somewhere to stay. I stepped back from a full-time role to do that properly: built a small software product, took on a few consulting engagements where AI did much of the heavy lifting, and learned something about myself along the way. I am better, and happier, building on something that already exists than starting from a blank page, and I like being accountable to people and having people accountable to me. So I am looking for one company to commit to for a long time. Offering a piece of the work up front is partly for you and partly for me: it is the most honest way I know to find out whether we would work well together.

Where it has worked before

This is how I was hired at Black & White Zebra. During the process I asked the kind of questions I am describing here and came back with a plan for the lead-generation program: what I thought was going on, what I would look at first, what I would change in the first few months. The executives told me afterwards that it was a large part of why they were comfortable bringing me on. It also turned out to be my own blueprint once I started; I followed it, with adjustments, for most of the first year, and the program grew about fourfold in the first half of that. That is one example, not a guarantee, but it is why I think the idea is worth offering.

What it would involve

One or two conversations, then a short written plan. You would tell me what is going well, what is getting in the way, and where you think the easier gains might be. I would ask the questions I would ask in a first week, and share how I see the revenue structure fitting together and where I would want to understand more. Then I would write it up: a first-quarter plan at 30, 60 and 90 days, the two or three things I would focus on first and why, what I would want to measure early, and what I would leave alone. We would talk it through, and you would have it in front of you when you decide.

It would not need access to your systems or data. It would work from what you are comfortable telling me and what is public, and I would be clear about where I am guessing. It is small by design: a few conversations and a document a few days after the last of them. And it is only an offer. If it does not fit how you hire, I am glad to go through the usual steps.

What you would have at the end

A plan you can keep, whatever you decide. A written view of your revenue operation from someone looking at it fresh, a set of questions that are often more useful than the answers, and a clearer sense of how I think when it is your business in front of me rather than a past one.

What I am looking for

A Director or Head of Revenue Operations or GTM Operations role at a B2B company, remote from Calgary, or hybrid where there is a Calgary office. Full-time, and for the long term.

If you would like to try it

Reply to whatever brought you here, or write to Zack.m.mcgill@gmail.com. I will send over the handful of questions I would want to start with, so you can see what we would talk about before anything is booked.
