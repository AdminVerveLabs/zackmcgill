A suggestion for how we might start

If you are considering me, I would like to offer something alongside the interviews.

Interviews are conversations in which I try to convince you I would be good at the job. I would like to add a small version of the job itself, on my own time, and let you judge from that. This page explains what I mean, why I am suggesting it, where it has worked before, and what it would and would not involve.

In short

If it would be useful, I would be glad to put together a short first-quarter plan for you as part of the process. It would come out of one or two conversations about what is going well and what is getting in the way, and you would have something concrete of mine to react to rather than only my answers to questions. It is how I was hired at Black & White Zebra, and the plan I wrote then turned out to be roughly the one I ran. It needs nothing from your systems, and if it does not fit how you hire, that is completely fine.

Why I am suggesting this

I spent the last year learning what AI changes about this work, and came out of it wanting a team and a long tenure. I stepped back from a full-time role to do that properly: built a software product of my own, took on a few consulting engagements where AI did much of the heavy lifting, and found I am better, and happier, building on something someone else has already built than starting from a blank page. I like being accountable to people and having people accountable to me. So I am looking for one company to commit to for a long time, and I would like the start of that relationship to feel like the relationship: you seeing the work before you decide, rather than hearing me describe it.

I have done this before

This is how I was hired at Black & White Zebra. During the process I asked the questions I am describing here and came back with a plan for the lead-generation program: what I thought was going on, what I would look at first, what I would change in the first few months. The executives told me afterwards it was a large part of why they were glad to bring me on. It was also my own blueprint once I started; I followed it, with adjustments, for roughly the first six months to a year; the program grew about fourfold inside the first half of that. So this is not an experiment. It is the way I already know works, for me and for the people deciding.

What I am proposing

Treat the first conversation or two the way I would treat a first consulting call. You tell me what is going well, what is getting in the way, and where you think the easiest gains are. I ask the questions I would ask on day one, then talk through what I see: how the revenue structure fits together, from the first signal of demand to the number you report, and where I would want to know more and what would tell me. Then I write it up: a first-quarter plan at 30, 60 and 90 days, the two or three things I would focus on first and why, what I would measure in the first month, and what I would leave alone. We walk through it together, and you decide with that in front of you.

It needs no access to your systems or data; it works from what you are comfortable telling me and what is public, and I will say where I am estimating. It is bounded: a few conversations and a written plan a few days after the last of them. And it is only an offer. If it does not fit how you hire, I am glad to go through the usual steps.

What you would have at the end

A plan you can keep, and a better read on me than an interview gives. Whatever you decide, you would have a written view of your revenue operation from someone looking at it fresh, a set of questions that are usually more useful than the answers, and a sense of how I think with your business in front of me rather than a past one.

What I am looking for

Head of Revenue at BeMo, specifically. The posting describes the job I want: own the whole revenue system, find the constraint before prescribing, run a disciplined testing program, build the forecast and the cadence, and lead the people who run it. Remote from Calgary. Full-time, for the long term.

If you would like to try it

Reply to whatever brought you here, or write to Zack.m.mcgill@gmail.com. I will send over the handful of questions I would want to start with, so you can see what we would talk about before we book anything.
