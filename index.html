Revenue operations · Calgary, AB

I build and run the operating system behind revenue, and I am looking for one company to do it in for a long time.

I am looking for one company where both the role and what the company does are things I would want to spend years on. This site is how I think about a revenue system, where I have run one, and how I would like to start.

I have worked most of the seats in a revenue organization, from the bottom up: I started as a BDR making cold calls, ran a 14-country territory with resellers in it, was the founding go-to-market hire at a bootstrapped software company where I also owned customer service, and then ran a revenue program end to end for a publisher, reporting to the CEO and COO. The last two years have been spent on the work that bears most directly on this role: building the systems, the measurement and the cadence that a revenue team runs on. This site shows how I do that, where I have done it recently, and a suggestion for how we might start.
