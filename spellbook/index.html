Revenue operations · Calgary, AB

I build and run the operating system behind revenue, and I am looking for one company to do it in for a long time.

This goes with my application for Head of Revenue Operations at Spellbook. Your posting asks for someone to own the operating model for the lead-to-closed-won engine and to trace a revenue target down to pipeline, capacity and productivity. This site is how I think about that model, four places where I have built parts of it, and a suggestion for how we might start.

I have worked most of the seats in a revenue organization, from the bottom up: cold-calling BDR, a 14-country territory with resellers in it, founding go-to-market hire at a bootstrapped company where I also owned customer service, and then a revenue program I ran end to end for a publisher, reporting to the CEO and COO. The parts of your posting I have done: segmentation, territory and capacity planning used to set quota; routing and a forecast held within ±10%; a Salesforce rebuild to one source of truth; the operating cadence and the executive review; and a testing program with hypotheses and guardrails. The parts I have not: run this for a team above ten sellers, or led an ops team of my own. I would rather say that here than have you find it in the second interview. The last two years have been spent building the measurement, systems and cadence that let a revenue engine run without depending on any one person, with AI doing the parts of the work it is now good at.
